# Student & Staff User Guide

Welcome to the ICT Helpdesk System! This guide will help you report issues and get technical support.

## Getting Started

### 1. Registration
- Navigate to the Helpdesk portal.
- Click **"Create an account"**.
- Fill in your details. You **must** use your `@university.edu.ng` email address.
- Your account will remain in a "Pending" state until an ICT Admin approves it. You will receive an email once approved.

### 2. Logging In
- Once approved, enter your university email and the password you created during registration.
- If you forget your password, contact the ICT department for a reset link.

---

## Reporting an Issue

1. Click on **"New Ticket"** in the top navigation bar.
2. Provide a clear, brief **Title** (e.g., "Cannot connect to WiFi in CS lab").
3. Select the appropriate **Category** and **Specific Issue**.
4. Choose a **Priority** level. Please reserve Critical priority for severe system-wide issues or urgent examination/CBT issues.
5. Provide your current **Location** (e.g., Room 204, Library).
6. Provide a detailed **Description** of the issue, any error messages, and what you were doing when it occurred.
7. Click **Submit Ticket**. You will receive a Ticket ID (e.g., `TKT-2026-0034`).

---

## Tracking Your Tickets

- Click **"My Tickets"** to view all issues you have reported.
- You can filter this list by status (Open, In Progress, Resolved).
- Click on any ticket to view details.

### Communicating with Technicians

- Inside a ticket, you can post comments in the **Discussion** section.
- Use this to answer questions from technicians or provide additional context.
- When an issue is fixed, the status will change to **Resolved**.

## Notifications

- The 🔔 bell icon in the top right shows unread notifications.
- You will be notified when:
  - Your ticket status changes.
  - A technician replies to your ticket.
- Click a notification to immediately jump to the relevant ticket.
