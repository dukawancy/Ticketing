# Technician / Unit Head Guide

Welcome to the technician portal. This guide explains how to manage your daily queue and communicate with users.

## The Technician Dashboard

- When you log in, your dashboard displays **My Open** and **My In Progress** tickets.
- It provides a prioritized list of tickets requiring your attention.

## Managing Your Work Queue

- To see everything assigned to you, click **My Tickets**.
- Tickets are sorted such that critical and overdue tickets appear first.

### Working on a Ticket
1. Click on a ticket in your queue.
2. Review the location and description.
3. If you need more information, use the **Discussion** box to leave a comment for the user.
4. **Internal Notes:** If you want to leave a note for other technicians or admins (which the student will *not* see), check the **Internal Note** box before posting your comment.

### Updating Status
As you work on the issue, keep the status updated using the dropdown on the right:
- **In Progress:** You are actively working on it.
- **Resolved:** You believe the issue is fixed.
- **Escalated:** You cannot solve the issue and require senior assistance.

## Unit Heads

If you are a Unit Head (e.g., Head of Networking):
- Your dashboard will show team workload statistics.
- You have access to the **All Tickets** navigation tab to view tickets falling under your unit.
- You can step in to reassign tickets or override statuses if SLA limits are nearing breach.
