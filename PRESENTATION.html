The Problem

Why This System Matters

Without a centralized ticketing system, universities face significant operational challenges in IT support delivery.

⏰

Slow Response

Issues reported via email, phone, or walk-in get lost, duplicated, or forgotten with no tracking mechanism.

📋

No Accountability

Without assignment tracking, it's impossible to know who is handling what, leading to duplicated effort or neglect.

📊

Zero Analytics

Management has no visibility into recurring issues, team performance, or SLA compliance without proper data.

05 / 12
Ticket Lifecycle

How Tickets Flow

Each ticket follows a structured lifecycle with built-in SLA monitoring.

1
Submit
Student or staff submits ticket with category, priority, and description
2
Triage
Dispatcher routes ticket to the appropriate ICT unit
3
Assign
Unit Head or Admin assigns the ticket to a specific technician
4
Work
Technician works on the issue, adds notes, updates status
5
Resolve
Issue fixed, user confirms resolution, ticket is closed
Priority Response Time Auto-Escalate Example
Critical 1 hour 2 hours CBT exam server down
High 4 hours 12 hours Faculty internet outage
Medium 24 hours 3 days Lab PC not working
Low 72 hours 7 days Software install request
